### The four rounds (each found real defects — the loop converging)
- **R1** — `_SEGMENT_SPLIT` enumerated `&&`/`||`/`;`/`|` and missed newline, single `&`, `|&`. The
  most routine multi-command form (a multi-line bash block) slipped past. Fixed as a definition of
  the complete top-level separator set.
- **R2** — the fix opened a hole: the heredoc-body stripper swallowed the whole rest of the line
  when a `<<` was not really a heredoc (`$((1<<2))`, a comment, a quoted `<<EOF`). "COMPLETE set"
  claim disproven (for/while/if/subshell/`$(…)` all pass — verb-position class). Fixed with the
  terminator condition; the residue named honestly.
- **R3** — a redirect line ending in `\` continuation bridged the excised body (hole); `)` in
  quoted prose opened a false name-position (false positive on `git commit -m "…(…)… capture"`);
  a help flag in a quoted value voided the line (hole). Root of the last two: the quote mask was
  not consulted.
- **R4** — `body = [""] * len(body)` (line-count preserved) closes the continuation bridge; a new
  quote-respecting tokenizer `_tokens` closes both quote-mask edges; the marker over-refusals named
  in L39. Verifier attacked `_tokens` at its edges (escaped quotes, `--host`/`-help` substrings,
  `--note=--help`, real case/function forms, tokenizer↔join order) — all held.

### The three named, user-approvable residue classes (L39, each measured, pinned, bounded)
1. **Verb position the reader does not follow** — wrapped shell (`sh -c`/`bash -lc`), substitution
   (`$(…)`/backtick/`<(…)`, incl. embedded in double quotes — bash runs it, the reader does not
   follow), unlisted wrapper (`nohup`/`timeout`/`uv run`/`xargs`/`eval`), encoded forms.
2. **Marker self-disable without the marker word** — reached via `mv`/glob/`find -delete`/
   substitution path, without its name appearing as a word.
3. **Unterminated heredoc** — an over-refusal (rc 2) as the deliberate price of the terminator
   condition.
